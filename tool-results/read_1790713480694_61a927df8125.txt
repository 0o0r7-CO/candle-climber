    13→    13→## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
    14→    14→
    15→    15→vibe/vibe is a **permissionless token launchpad built by the Seedify ecosystem on Robinhood Chain** (an Arbitrum-based Ethereum L2 by Robinhood the brokerage) [S5][S22][S24]. Its incentivized testnet is live at `testnet.vibevibe.fun` — users discover, test-trade and launch tokens with **no real funds at risk** [S1]. The protocol runs a bonding-curve engine ("seedify-curve-v3") with **automatic buyback & burn** wired into every token by default, 125 bps fees split 75/25 between creators and protocol, and graduation to a Uniswap-v4 pool at 5 ETH net [S2][S15].
    16→    16→
    17→    17→The strategic moment: the founder **@meta_alchemist** announced (2026-09-23) that the leaderboard structure is shifting to **three boards — meme & artcoins / infra & product / stock & rwa** — and that **most of the 5% token supply reserved for testnet rewards will go to quality builder projects** [S12]. His explicitly recommended "sweet spot" is the **merge of all three categories**: *"a product or a game or something useful that integrates stock pairs, and has some of storytelling/creative aspect to it too"* [S12]. A parallel official tweet defines an upcoming **"agent quant office"** — agentic wallets, copytrading, gamified leaderboards [S15].
    18→    18→
    19→    19→Robinhood Chain itself carries **190+ tokenized real-world instruments on mainnet, including stock tokens (TSLA, AAPL, AMZN, NFLX, S&P 500)**, with mock stock tokens available on testnet via the faucet [S25][S9][S19]. This makes "game × stock pairs × storytelling" a uniquely buildable niche **right now, on testnet, with near-zero cost**.
    20→    20→
    21→    21→**Agent directive distilled:** design and ship a quality product in the founder's stated merge-zone, launch it as a token on the vibe/vibe testnet bonding curve, register it through the official showcase + builder-role flow, and accumulate legitimate, reviewable activity — while structurally avoiding every anti-abuse trigger the program documents [S6][S7][S17].
